What are “The Bestbois”?

“The Bestbois” is a group primarily based out of VRChat and similar VR games. We are one of the oldest currently running VRChat furry communities, with ties dating back to early 2018.
These days we mainly operate out of our Discord server. Despite the big size, the community is tight-knit, but still very welcoming to newcomers.
Our models are created by our dedicated Bestboi creation team, funded by our Patreon and other sources. We are dedicated to creating high-quality, easy to edit, and fully customizable free models.
